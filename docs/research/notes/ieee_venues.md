Research date: 2026-10-24. Submission window of interest: Nov 2026 to Sep 2027. Any date marked **(INFERRED)** is projected from the previous edition's timeline and was NOT confirmed for the 2026/27 cycle.

## Q1. IEEE-sponsored or co-sponsored conferences in India (Karnataka/Bengaluru first), plus IEEE IEEM and INDICON

### Takeaway
The best fits whose deadlines fall in the window are: IEEE CONECCT 2027 (IEEE Bangalore Section, Bengaluru; 2026 deadline was 9 Feb, and it has a 4-page Work-in-Progress track open to everyone); RVCE's own CSITSS (6 pages, deadline around late July in 2024); IEEE IEEM 2027 (the industrial-engineering flagship, IEEE Xplore; 2026 full-paper deadline was 1 Jul); and IEEE INDICON 2027 (2026 deadline was 31 Aug). None of the 2027 editions had published a call for papers that I could find.

## Q2. Undergrad-specific options (IEEE student contests, IEOM, IISE) suitable for a first-year

### Takeaway
IISE and IEOM both run competitions open specifically to undergraduates where the student is first author. IISE's cycle opens with abstracts around 10 November, so the 2027 cycle is probably closing soon (inferred). IEOM runs undergraduate paper competitions at its 2027 conferences in Kuala Lumpur, Bogor, Ghana and Boston, all with onsite and online participation, but none of the pages fetched gave dates or fees.

### Cited Findings
- **IISE OR Division Undergraduate Student Research Dissemination competition:** the first author must be an undergraduate. The work must be predominantly the student's own, faculty may be co-authors, and the research must be original and not entered in another competition. Process: abstract, then a full paper under IISE proceedings guidelines, double-blind review, and in-person finals. 2025–26 dates: abstracts closed 10 Nov 2025; full paper due 25 Jan 2026 (another section of the page says 18 Jan); finals at the IISE Annual Conference, 16–19 May 2026, Arlington, Texas. — [IISE OR Division competition](https://www.iise.org/Details.aspx?id=38059)
- **IISE Lean Systems Track Best Student Paper (OpEx Division):** open to full-time undergraduate or graduate students. The paper must be accepted in the proceedings, and the advisor must email a statement confirming the student did the work. Topics are lean work such as simulation, implementation and kaizen, which fits WIP limits. Same 2025–26 dates (abstract 10 Nov 2025, full paper 25 Jan 2026). — [IISE Lean competition](https://www.iise.org/Details.aspx?id=4042)
- Other IISE student paper competitions: [SEMS](https://www.iise.org/Details.aspx?id=22244), [Sustainable Development](https://iise.org/Details.aspx?id=37912), [Work Systems](https://www.iise.org/Details.aspx?id=50011)
- **IISE 2027 (INFERRED):** abstracts around 10 Nov 2026, full paper around late Jan 2027, finals around May 2027 in the US. Not confirmed.
- **IEOM undergraduate paper competitions (2027):** open to students or recent graduates. Course projects, internship work or research all qualify. The student must be first author, with faculty acting only as advisors. The paper must follow the IEOM template and be presented at that conference. At the Kuala Lumpur conference, winning papers are published in the proceedings. — [IEOM Kuala Lumpur 2027](https://ieomsociety.org/malaysia2027/undergraduate-paper-competition/), [IEOM Boston 2027](https://ieomsociety.org/boston2027/undergraduate-paper-competition/), [IEOM Bogor 2027](https://ieomsociety.org/bogor2027/undergraduate-paper-competition/), [IEOM Ghana 2027](https://ieomsociety.org/ghana2027/undergraduate-paper-competition/). None of these pages listed deadlines, fees or indexing.

### Inferences
- IEOM's online-participation option and its "course project" eligibility make it the easiest undergrad competition for a first-year student in India to enter. IISE requires in-person finals in the US, which is costly.
- The IISE Lean track's scope (lean, kaizen, simulation) matches a WIP-cap alert project well.

### Gaps
- The IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific) Student Paper Contest and IEEE Bangalore Section student paper contests were not researched within the tool budget. Check the IEEE R10 site.
- IEOM 2027 conference dates, deadlines, fees and Scopus indexing were not found.

## Q3. Predatory "IEEE" conferences and how to verify sponsorship

### Takeaway
Use IEEE's own tools (IEEE Conference Search on events.ieee.org and the Author Center's "Find an IEEE Conference") to confirm a conference is listed. Also check that the sponsor's own website links back to the conference. IEEE requires in-person presentation for a paper to appear in Xplore, Scopus or Web of Science.

### Cited Findings
- IEEE's Author Center points authors to the IEEE Publication Recommender to find conferences. It also states that "IEEE expects authors to present their papers in person at the conference." Presentation is required for inclusion in IEEE Xplore and in indexes such as Web of Science and Scopus. — [IEEE Author Center: Find an IEEE Conference](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-conference-author/find-an-ieee-conference/)
- IEEE Conference Search tools: [events.ieee.org search](https://events.ieee.org/organize-an-event/search-ieee-conferences/), [IEEE Conference Search news post](https://events.ieee.org/news/find-upcoming-virtual-events-with-ieee-conference-search/)
- Some societies distinguish technical co-sponsorship from full sponsorship. — [IEEE Education Society: Technical Co-Sponsorship](https://ieee-edusociety.org/conferences/about-conferences/technical-co-sponsorship-conferences)
- Red flags listed in the CSUN library guide:
  - The sponsoring organization's site doesn't link to the conference, or committee members never agreed to serve.
  - The organizer is a for-profit company.
  - The scope is vague or very broad.
  - The same event recurs in many cities, or several "conferences" share one venue and date.
  - Acceptance is very fast (around 24 hours) or the review process is unclear.
  - Fees are high, or presenters pay more than attendees.
  - Invitations are flattering and full of grammar errors.
  - The website has no past proceedings.
  — [CSUN predatory conferences guide](https://libguides.csun.edu/predatory_publishing/conferences)
- Similar guidance: [Deakin library guide](https://deakin.libguides.com/your-publishing-plan/conferences), [Enago Q&A](https://www.enago.com/academy/q/11711/i-have-received-invitation-from-a-conference-how-do-i-find-out-whether-it-is-predatory-or-not/)
- Legitimate events can post their CFP on IEEE infrastructure, for example IEEE vTools listings such as [ICCSIC 2026 on vTools](https://events.vtools.ieee.org/event/register/532807). A vTools listing alone is not proof of sponsorship; that is an inference.

### Inferences
- Practical check: (1) find the event in IEEE Conference Search and note its IEEE conference record number; (2) look for past proceedings of the same series in IEEE Xplore; (3) confirm the IEEE Section or Society page (for example ieeebangalore.org or the IEEE India Council) links to it; (4) be wary of "IEEE-style" events run by private firms in many cities, promising Scopus indexing with quick acceptance.
- "Submitted to IEEE Xplore" (INDICON's wording) means publication is not guaranteed and depends on presenting the paper and passing quality checks.

### Gaps
- The IEEE Conference Search page itself was not fetched, so its exact search fields are unconfirmed.

## Q4. Usual IEEE paper structure expectations

### Takeaway
IEEE conference papers use the two-column IEEE conference template (A4 or US Letter as the venue specifies) and usually run 4–8 pages including references. Short, WIP or student papers are about 4 pages. The usual structure is IMRaD: Abstract, Index Terms, Introduction, Related Work, Method, Results, Conclusion, References. References use IEEE numbered style ([1], [2]...).

### Cited Findings
- CONECCT: page limits *include* figures, tables and references. Research track 6–8 pages; WIP/position track 4 pages. — [CONECCT 2026 CFP](https://2026.connect.ieeebangalore.org/?p=37)
- CSITSS: up to 6 pages, A4, PDF. — [CSITSS-2024 flyer](https://rvce.edu.in/sites/default/files/CSITSS-2024-n.pdf)
- IEEM: use the official IEEM paper template; abstract-only submissions are allowed but are not published in Xplore. — [IEEM 2026 CFP](https://mie.binus.ac.id/?p=73667)
- The IEEE Author Center links to "Authoring Tools and Templates" under "Write Your Paper." — [IEEE Author Center](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-conference-author/find-an-ieee-conference/)
- Examples of conference author guidelines using the IEEE template: [COMPSAC paper formats](https://ieeecompsac.computer.org/2021/paper-formats/), [Editage: how to write a conference paper](https://www.editage.com/blog/how-to-write-a-conference-paper-format-examples-and-templates/)

### Inferences
- For a first-year student's short paper, aim for 4 pages:
  - Problem: WIP build-up and the cost of monitoring
  - Related work on WIP caps (CONWIP, kanban) and low-cost IoT monitoring
  - System design (sensors or data, alert logic)
  - Evaluation on a public manufacturing dataset, with metrics
  - Limitations
  - About 15–25 IEEE-style references
- Many venues check PDFs with IEEE PDF eXpress and screen for plagiarism. This is a general norm and was not confirmed on the pages fetched.

- The exact IEEE template specifications (fonts, margins) and PDF eXpress requirements were not fetched from IEEE directly.
